Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Purpose

Metaseed Hub is a collaborative platform for creating and managing scientific metadata. This policy defines acceptable use of the service to ensure a productive and secure environment for all users.

2. Intended Use

Metaseed Hub is intended for:

  • Creating and managing scientific metadata following standard schemas (MIAPPE, ISA, DiSSCo, Darwin Core)
  • Collaborating with colleagues on research data documentation
  • Exporting metadata for publication and data sharing
  • Academic and research purposes

3. User Responsibilities

As a user, you agree to:

  • Use the service only for legitimate research and academic purposes
  • Keep your login credentials secure and not share them
  • Respect the intellectual property rights of others
  • Not upload sensitive personal data (health records, financial data) unless authorized
  • Report security vulnerabilities or suspicious activity
  • Comply with applicable laws and TU Delft policies

4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to accounts or data
  • Uploading malware, viruses, or malicious content
  • Using the service for commercial purposes without authorization
  • Harassing, threatening, or abusing other users
  • Violating privacy laws or data protection regulations
  • Circumventing security measures or access controls
  • Using automated tools to scrape or overload the service

5. Data and Content

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of metadata you enter
  • Do not upload data that infringes copyright or other rights
  • Ensure you have appropriate permissions for any data you enter
  • Consider data sensitivity when sharing workspaces with collaborators

6. Service Availability

While we strive for high availability:

  • The service is provided "as is" without guarantees
  • Scheduled maintenance may cause temporary unavailability
  • We recommend regular exports of important data

7. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Warning and request to cease the activity
  • Temporary suspension of access
  • Permanent termination of access
  • Referral to appropriate authorities for legal violations

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Significant changes will be communicated to users via email or in-application notification.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or to report violations:

Email: metaseed-support@tudelft.nl
